Outdoor Adventure Center · Detroit, MI

Ship's Bridge Simulator

The Outdoor Adventure Center wanted visitors to feel what it takes to steer a real ship. So we're building them a bridge: three screens for windows, a physical wheel and throttle wired through custom sensors, and open water simulated in Unreal Engine — wind, wake, and momentum included. I scoped it, and I'm directing the build.

Field note

Sensor input gets filtered and remapped in-engine, so a kid slamming the throttle reads as intent. The sea itself is a real-time water particle system. And because the side screens angle in like a real bridge, it renders through nDisplay — Unreal's way of drawing a correctly-angled view for each screen instead of one stretched image.

Unreal Engine + nDisplay (real-time 3D) · three-screen bridge · custom wheel + throttle sensors

U.S. Space & Rocket Center · Huntsville, AL

How We Know the Weather

A 3,500 square feet of live science at the U.S. Space & Rocket Center: a 24'×14' LED wall streaming real Baron weather feeds (the same ones you might see on TV behind wather broadcasters), and 54 screens of hands-on games — in front of 800,000 visitors a year.

Field note

I carried this from concept through install — building the content and project-managing the content teams. The wall runs my HTML layout through a a video processor that crashes on the wrong resolution, so every block is pinned to its exact pixel region, and a dropped feed fails quietly instead of taking the wall down.

Live NASA / NOAA / Baron data · 24'×14' LED wall · 54 screens · web-based interactives

Toyota Experience Center · San Antonio, TX

Toyota North America

Six applications and six video channels act as a single synchronized piece, built end to end inside Toyota's design system. One simple story about people, places, and cars.

Field note

The apps stay communicate with each other over WebSockets — a live connection that lets every screen react in the same instant. It's the most complex build in the Center, and it reads as one simple story.

Six synced apps · WebSockets (real-time sync) · six video channels

03 / About

Christian Lozano CH Experiences LLC

I design it, build it, and stand next to it when the doors open.

The software side of exhibits is my whole job — web applications, games, interactive maps, video. The work is live at five Toyota experience centers across four states (60,000+ sq ft combined), the U.S. Space & Rocket Center, and Detroit's Outdoor Adventure Center.

Most of it is judgment: what belongs on a screen, what belongs in the room, and what a visitor should feel in the few seconds they give you. I prototype in days, not weeks — AI-assisted where it earns its place — then harden the work for years of public use.

Tools I reach for — React · Node.js · Unreal Engine · BrightSign · ArcGIS · AWS · After Effects · Premiere
